Tint

Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Tint is a daily color game for iPhone. It doesn't collect any data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app doesn't connect to the internet.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, Tint keeps a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • The colors you lock in: the date, the color and its name, the drops you used and your match.
  • Your mix in progress for today, so you can come back to it.
  • Your settings: the daily reminder and its time, and haptics.

Tint's widgets read a small summary of this (today's color, your streak, your last seven days) from storage shared between the app and its widgets on the same device. None of it is sent anywhere. It is included in your device backups like any app's data, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

What Tint doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising, and no third-party code that does.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No data sold or shared, because none is collected.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, Tint asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your iPhone. They are local notifications: no server is involved. You can turn them off in Tint's settings or in the Settings app.

Sharing

When you tap Share result, iOS shows its share sheet with an image of your result card and a short line of text. They go only where you choose to send them. The card shows your match and number of drops, never your recipe. If you choose Save Image, iOS asks for permission to add the image to your photos. Tint can only add images; it can't see your photo library.

Children

Tint is suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
