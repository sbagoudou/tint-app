Tint

Support

Tint is a daily color game for iPhone. Every day there is one new color. Mix it from four paints, one drop at a time, and make the seam between your mix and the target disappear.

Questions

When does the next color arrive?

At midnight, your local time. Everyone gets the same color on the same date.

Can I play a day I missed, or try again?

Not in this version. There is one try a day, and a missed day stays as a gap in your swatch book.

If I leave in the middle of a mix, is it lost?

No. Your drops are kept until midnight, so you can come back and keep mixing.

How does my streak work?

It counts the days in a row you locked in a mix. If you haven't played today yet, yesterday still counts, so your streak is waiting, not lost.

The daily reminder doesn't show up

Check that Daily reminder is on in Tint's settings, and that notifications for Tint are allowed in the Settings app under Notifications. Reminders only come on days you haven't mixed yet.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Tint. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, then the Lock Screen, and add Tint from the widget list.

How do I start over?

Your swatch book lives only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.
